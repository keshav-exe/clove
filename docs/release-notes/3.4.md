Clove 3.4 shows what's using your Mac, finds what's safe to delete, and is much lighter on your battery.

- **Activity:** see what's running and what it costs. Apps are counted with all their helper processes, so Cursor is one row, not forty, and `claude` in a terminal shows as Claude Code. Quit an app or stop a command from the list, and see how warm your Mac is and what Clove itself is using.
- **Safe to delete:** Storage now lists leftover data you can remove without worrying: Xcode build files, old device support, simulator caches, package downloads, app logs, old installers, and `node_modules` in projects you haven't touched in months. Each one says what it is and what happens after, and everything goes to the Trash first.
- **Lighter:** Clove reads your processes straight from macOS instead of starting `ps` and `lsof` every few seconds, the notch no longer redraws while agents work, and pages stop refreshing when the window is closed. Idle, it uses about a quarter of what 3.3 did.
- **The real app in the notch:** a Claude model running in T3 Code shows T3 Code. Agents in several apps fan out as overlapping icons.
- **Click to open the chat:** clicking an agent in the notch opens its conversation: the project's window in Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf or Zed, the exact tab in Terminal or iTerm, or the app itself.
- **One notification per task:** the notch tells you when an agent's whole task is done, not every time it pauses for background work. It says when an agent has a question or stopped on an error, groups agents that finish together, and stays quiet when you're already looking at the app.
- **Tidier sidebar:** This Mac holds Activity, Storage, and Worktrees; Cleanup moved in with Skills. The notch drops its Cleanup tab.
