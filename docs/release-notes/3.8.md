Clove 3.8 lets you pick the mark beside the notch while an agent works, opens a half-open notch when you rest on it, and does less in the background.

- **Working mark:** Settings › General › Working mark picks what sits beside the notch while one agent works: the still Dot, as before, or Breathe, Orbit, Bars or Ripple. The moving ones are drawn by macOS itself, so Clove doesn't redraw every frame, and they hold still with Reduce Motion.
- **Open on hover, everywhere:** when an agent starts or finishes and the notch grows a little, resting the pointer on it opens the rest. Announcements with their own button, like a question to answer, still wait for your click.
- **Settings that look like the notch:** the pictures in Settings now show the real island, its cards, icons and tabs, instead of grey placeholders.
- **Cursor limits:** Plan limits can show Cursor's month alongside Claude and Codex, when Cursor's sign-in is one Clove can read. Newer Cursor builds encrypt it, and Clove won't take Cursor's key from your Keychain, so there it simply isn't listed.
- **Lighter in the background:** Clove no longer wakes every second and a half to look for the active display unless you've asked the island to follow it across more than one screen.
