Clove 3.1 brings your containers into Running, puts a live number in the menu bar, and makes every clear undoable.

- **Containers:** Running now lists your Docker containers, from Docker Desktop, OrbStack, Colima, or Rancher Desktop. Start, stop, or restart one, or open it in your browser when it has a port open. The menu bar panel's Running block shows them too. Without Docker, nothing changes.
- **A number in the menu bar:** show free space, dev servers, all servers, or containers beside the icon. Pick it in Settings, General, under "Beside the icon". At zero, only the icon shows.
- **A smoother panel:** the menu bar panel opens like a popover, growing from the menu bar with a short spring, and fades out. With Reduce Motion on, it just appears.
- **Nothing you can't get back:** clearing caches moves them to the Trash first, and Recently cleared puts any of it back until you empty the Trash. Only Empty Trash deletes for good, and it asks first.
- **Always keep:** mark a folder, a skill, or a command and Clove never clears or suggests it again, in any future scan.
- **Home starts with your Mac:** the live storage bar, then what needs a look, including the permissions Clove works better with and when your disk is nearly full.
- **Easier to read:** stronger contrast in light and dark, a softer dark background, plainer labels, and the All Skills row keeps its icon when selected.
