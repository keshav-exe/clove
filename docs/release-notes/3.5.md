Clove 3.5 tells you which app is holding a port, shows how much of your Claude and Codex plans you've used, and lets you lay out the notch's Now tab your way.

- **Every port, named:** a port an app keeps open for itself now says whose it is. `:7265` is Raycast, `:44950` is Figma, `:5000` is AirPlay Receiver. Servers you start stay dev servers, even from Cursor's terminal, and Postgres, Redis, nginx, ngrok and cloudflared now count as dev servers too. Activity gets an App Ports section.
- **Choose which ports show:** Settings › Now has switches for dev servers, ports apps keep open, and ports macOS keeps open. Hover a port in the notch and click Hide to stop listing that app. AirPlay Receiver gets a Free button that opens the setting that frees `:5000`.
- **Claude and Codex limits:** see how much of each plan's five-hour and weekly windows you've used, and when each resets. A faint tick marks how much of the window has gone by, so you can tell whether you'll run out before it resets. The notch grows when a limit is nearly used up. It's off until you turn it on: Clove reads each agent's sign-in, and macOS asks first.
- **Now, your way:** show, hide and reorder Now's sections (agents, servers and ports, limits, busy apps, MCP servers and containers) in Settings › Now, or right-click any of them in the notch.
- **Glassier notch:** the open island is more see-through, its cards are clear glass, and an agent at work breathes a soft glow along the bottom of its card, in the colours of the app it's running in.
- **Right-click works in the notch:** copy a server's URL, reveal its folder, stop it, or open it in Activity.
