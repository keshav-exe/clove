Clove 3.9 makes the notch open and close smoothly, and lets you choose how much glass you see through it.

- **Smoother open and close:** the notch now grows as one clean black shape. What's inside stays in place and fades in from a soft blur, instead of sliding in from the sides. Closing tucks it straight back, with no grey flash on the way.
- **Glass that follows your Mac:** with Liquid Glass on, Match macOS uses the Clear or Tinted look from System Settings › Appearance. Turn it off to pick your own spot on the Tint slider, which runs clear to tinted the same way as the system's and settles on a midpoint.
- **Clear that stays readable:** toward Clear, the cards in the notch carry their own frost, the way Control Center's tiles do. The desktop shows through around them while their text stays easy to read. Toward Tinted, the notch fades from solid black into glass.
- **Settings in plain words:** every option in Settings now says what it does for you, without describing how Clove works inside.
