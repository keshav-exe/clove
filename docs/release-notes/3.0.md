Clove 3.0 adds Storage, makes the menu bar panel yours, and gives every section the same calm page.

- **Storage:** your disk as a live, dithered bar: apps, code and toolchains, photos, movies, documents, music, system data, caches, and the Trash. Rest on a part to see what's in it; the biggest items are listed below. Clear caches (open apps and macOS's own are left alone) or empty the Trash, with a confirmation first.
- **Menu bar blocks:** choose what the panel holds. Skills, Storage, Running, Cleanup, and Trending each have a tile along the bottom; ⌘1 to ⌘5 switch. Settings, General lists them too.
- **One page per section:** a sidebar and one page. Lists open what you pick in an inspector beside them, overviews scroll as one page, and everything follows light and dark.
- **Menu bar menu:** every section, the panel shortcut shown the native way, and badges for Cleanup and Storage.
- **Shortcut recorder:** the native recorder from Shotput. The current shortcut is released while you record, so it can't fire.
- **Lighter and faster:** the disk walk is about 3.7× faster and runs at low priority, a scan is remembered for 30 minutes, and animations stop once they settle.
- **Safer clears:** every delete is checked when it happens, links are never followed, and whole agent folders like `~/.claude` are never offered for the Trash. The last scan is kept in a private file, not your preferences.
